Our Legacy & Ethos

A Tradition of Intellectual & Moral Brilliance

Founded with a vision to provide world-class boarding education, Aurelia Heights Academy combines academic rigor with character building, ensuring every student discovers their highest potential.

Our Mission

To empower students through an exemplary, well-rounded curriculum that fosters critical thinking, creative innovation, global consciousness, and unyielding integrity within a supportive boarding environment.

Our Vision

To be recognized globally as a premier secondary institution that produces confident, compassionate, and visionary leaders ready to shape a dynamic world.

Academic Excellence

Instilling a relentless passion for inquiry, intellectual curiosity, and scholarly mastery across all disciplines.

Uncompromising Integrity

Cultivating honesty, moral courage, accountability, and empathy in everyday actions and community life.

Global Citizenship

Promoting cultural awareness, inclusivity, environmental stewardship, and a perspective that embraces diversity.

Resilience & Growth

Encouraging grit, physical wellness, adaptability, and the confidence to turn challenges into lifelong strengths.

World-Class Curriculum

Designed for Future Scholars

Our dual pathway academic framework enables students to excel in national and international examinations with ease.

Foundational Years

Building Strong Academic Foundations

The Junior Secondary program focuses on conceptual clarity, creative exploration, and essential life skills. Students engage with fundamental sciences, literature, mathematics, foreign languages, and digital literacy.

  • Comprehensive inquiry-based learning framework
  • Foreign languages: French, Spanish, & Mandarin options
  • Dedicated study skills & personal development coaching
Specialization Pathways

Rigorous Advanced Academics

Senior Secondary students select specialized tracks across Sciences, Social Sciences, Arts, and Business Studies, preparing them for top global universities and competitive examinations.

  • Tailored learning pathways according to career aspirations
  • SAT, IGCSE, and National Board exam preparation
  • Individualized mentorship from senior academic advisors
Future Readiness

Robotics, Coding & Scientific Inquiry

Our state-of-the-art Innovation Hub equips students with practical skills in Artificial Intelligence, Robotics, 3D Design, Applied Physics, and Environmental Biotech.

  • Hands-on AI & Python programming modules
  • Annual International Robotics Competitions
  • Advanced Science Laboratories with high-grade instruments
Pre-University Program

Gateway to World Premier Universities

Designed for high achievers, our A-Levels program offers intense focus in chosen subjects alongside comprehensive university application counseling for Ivy League and Russell Group institutions.

  • Dedicated UCAS and Common App guidance counselors
  • Internship placement programs during vacation periods
  • Mock interviews and scholarship preparation workshops
